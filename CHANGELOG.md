Field Twin changelog

2026-10-29, v2026.10.29. Export, mileage, and search spreadsheets now save into Downloads in the phone app. Added Daily miles, which shows start, end, and business miles for each workday from a start point shared by all phones. Stops can be marked as tickets, and confirmed days lock and save to a spreadsheet. Added a Daily miles report. Added GO, which gives turn by turn routes to a place you say or type, with logged places first.

2026-10-25, v2026.10.25. Added end of day debriefs. A prose debrief is written from the day log, and replies are saved with the log.

2026-10-23, v2026.10.23. Added an X on the odometer lock that puts the reading off for half an hour and pauses nags until then.

2026-10-21, v2026.10.21. Phones now show on the map in their own colors, with a shared home for stops and odometer prompts. The timeline uses half hour blocks, and a stop seen by several phones is kept once. Battery use dropped because GPS turns off while parked. The receipt reader retries through network hiccups.

2026-10-20, v2026.10.20. Added an Android app with auto tracking, automatic stops, GPS miles, and odometer prompts that lock the app. Added a live GPS dot and GPS calibration from odometer readings. One sign in now works across Field Twin Teal, Violet, and Glaucous. The receipt reader finds the account login after the camera.

2026-10-18, v2026.10.18. Saving a receipt now checks for duplicates, and likely duplicates are listed on the Spend page.

2026-10-17, v2026.10.17. Field Twin family links now point at Field Twin Glaucous. The Roads tab uses the same Field map.

2026-10-15, v2026.10.15. Added road hazards for crashes, construction, and fatal crash history, limited to the Las Vegas area. Added a day log, Field map and Inventory tabs, and password sign in. Deletes now carry across phones. The blue location dot refreshes about every five minutes. The menu was regrouped with short names.

2026-10-12, v2026.10.12. Added a road quality and travel heat map and a link to Chassisquiver. Rewrote the info page to cover every feature.

2026-10-07, v2026.10.07. Added log and receipt buttons, a road sign look, a thumb dock, marker clustering, and a grouped menu. Added odometer sampling and a mileage page, entry deleting, and a choice of pin location for receipts. Merged account and profile.

2026-10-06, v2026.10.06. Added sign in and cloud sync. Added a search page with filters, totals, a map view, and spreadsheet export. Every entry now has an edit button and notes, and receipts land on the timeline by printed date. Pins can be moved by tapping the map.

2026-09-29, v2026.09.29. Added receipt capture with a spend summary. The receipt address now overrides the phone location. The reader uses a vision service with an offline fallback, and a service check shows why a fallback happened.

2026-09-14, v2026.09.14. The map now shows whether each camera is online.

2026-09-09, v2026.09.09. First release. Added camera markers with icons and roadway labels on the map.
