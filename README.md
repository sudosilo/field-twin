Field Twin

Field Twin is a phone friendly field log. It is for a person who drives to job sites and needs to record stops, receipts, and miles.

A map shows entries, traffic cameras, and road hazards. Buttons log miles and receipts, and a receipt reader fills in the entry from a photo. Search, history, spend, and mileage views look things up. Sign in syncs entries across phones. An Android app tracks trips and stops automatically and asks for odometer readings. End of day debriefs summarize the day.

Live at field-twin-teal.vercel.app.
